## ❯ SO... WHAT IS MUSICX?

Basically, I got tired of music apps making everything unnecessarily annoying.

You just wanna listen to your own music and suddenly there's:
* A subscription
* An ad
* A "Start Free Trial" button
* An account you never wanted
* Some random cloud service
* 500 recommendations for songs you don't care about

Like bro, I just wanted to play a song.

So I made MusicX.

It's an **offline-first Android music player** made for your local music library.

> Your music should stay yours.

Put your music on your phone, open MusicX, and just listen.

No internet required.  
No subscription required.  
No account required.  

Just music.
