## ❯ Why I Built This

Most music apps are doing the absolute most.

Ads.  
Premium popups.  
“Sign in to continue.”  
“Upgrade to unlock basic functionality.” 💀  

I just wanted to press play and hear music.

Oh, and don’t even get me started on those “free” texting apps like TextMe or TextNow 😭  
Open the app → watch 3 ads → maybe send one message → watch another ad.

I did **not** want that kind of energy anywhere near my precious baby.

So I made MusicX.

It opens.  
It scans your songs.  
It plays them.

Crazy concept, I know.

---

## ❯ What You Get

- Fully offline playback  
- Local music scanning  
- Fast search for songs, artists, and metadata  
- Playlist support  
- Lyrics support through embedded lyrics and LRCLIB  
- Background playback  
- Lock screen and notification controls  
- Clean, minimal UI  
- Advanced color customization  

No streaming.  
No cloud.  
No tracking.  
No account creation side quest.

Zero nonsense 💀

---

## ❯ Customization

You’re not stuck with one boring theme.

You can tweak pretty much every color in the app — backgrounds, text, accents, player UI, buttons, and more.

If you want your music player pure black and minimal, you can.

If you want it looking like a neon science experiment, you can also do that.

If you spend 40 minutes adjusting colors instead of listening to music, that’s on you 😭

---

## ❯ How It Works

- Scans your music using Android’s MediaStore  
- Everything stays on your device  
- Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose  
- Uses Media3 / ExoPlayer for playback  

No weird background stuff.  
No mystery data flying to space.  
No “we value your privacy” popup doing the opposite 💀
